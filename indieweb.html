Got a little website of your own, on Neocities or anywhere else? Here are two ways to put ADV on it. Copy the code, paste it into your page's HTML, and that's it: no sign-up, nothing to download.

An 88x31 button

The classic little web button. It links to ADV, so people on your site can find it.

ADV Lite

A little ADV handheld that lives on your page and plays ADV's online games: tap a cart and it starts. There are no editors and no saving carts to the computer: just the games. New games show up by themselves whenever someone makes one.

Good to know

Make it smaller or bigger by changing the width and height: ADV Lite fits itself into whatever space it gets. 240 x 450 is nice and small.

Click it first, then the arrow keys and Z / X work. On a phone, use its buttons.

ADV Lite always says who made ADV underneath (that's atomicdude). Please leave that in!

Want your own game in it? Make one in the Developer Kit and send it in.
