ESC opens the editors. CTRL+R runs your cart. Your carts stay in this browser.

The easy way

1. In the sprite editor, pick a sprite and click "IT IS..." to say what it is: player, enemy, collect, solid, hurts or goal.

2. Paint them on the map. 3. Press CTRL+R. That's a game!

The advanced way

Write Lua in the code editor, make sounds and music, or mix in Scratch-style blocks. A new cart starts with a little level to play with.

Sharing it

Upload sends your cart to ADV's online carts from your own GitHub account. The owner of ADV takes a look, and then it's on everyone's screen. No GitHub? Download the picture and share that instead.
