A little fantasy console. Games are pictures.
Click a cart on the screen, then type RUN. Arrows, Z and X play. ESC stops.
ADV for Windows

The whole console: the cart library, the editors, the lot.

Unzip it, run ADV.exe. If Windows asks: "More info", then "Run anyway".
ADV for Android

A Game Boy-style handheld for playing carts on your phone.

Open the file on your phone and let it install.
The Cart Forge

Make your own carts right here, then send them to ADV online.

Easy: say what each sprite is and paint a map. Or write Lua.

Share a cart

Every ADV cart is a picture. Send one to a friend, and they can open it here, in the app, or even from a photo of it.
