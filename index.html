ADV

A little fantasy console. 128x128 pixels, 24 colors, and games that are pictures.

Click a cart on the screen (or drag it to the slot), then type RUN. Arrows, Z and X to play. ESC stops a game.

ADV for Windows The whole console: the cart library, the editors, the lot. Unzip it and run ADV.exe. Windows might say it doesn't know the app: click "More info", then "Run anyway". ADV for Android A Game Boy-style handheld for playing carts. Open the file on your phone and let your browser install apps when it asks. The Cart Forge Make your own carts right in the browser, then send them to ADV's online carts. Easy: say what each sprite is and paint a map. Advanced: Lua code, sounds and music.

Share a cart

Every ADV cart is a picture. Send one to a friend, and they can open it here, in the app, or even from a photo of it.
